# NEXUS ROLE / ACTION / RESOURCE MATRIX v1.0

**POLICY CONTRACT — NOT PRODUCTION SECURITY**

This document defines intended access boundaries for the eleven current runtime roles. It does not assert that current application authorization enforces them. Server-side/RLS enforcement will be implemented in later phases. Hiding a button is not authorization.

## Legend

- **Own** — only the user’s own records or records they created.
- **Assigned** — only resources explicitly assigned to the actor.
- **Org-scoped** — only records in an authorized organization/branch; organization model is **DECISION REQUIRED**.
- **Public** — public/searchable records subject to publication/verification policy.
- **Eligible only** — action requires an approved eligibility rule; exact evidence may remain **DECISION REQUIRED**.
- **Review only** — inspect/flag/recommend/escalate; does not mean final authority.
- **Human approval** — authorized human decision with reason and audit event.
- **No grant** — no intended grant is defined by this contract.
- **DECISION REQUIRED** — exact action/scope has not been approved.

## Cross-role constraints

1. **CANONICAL:** Runtime roles remain exactly Buyer, Seller, Agent, Agency, Developer, Investor, Industrial, Corporate, Government, AI Admin and Super Admin.
2. **CANONICAL:** Current runtime uses one primary role. Multi-role is **FUTURE ARCHITECTURE / DECISION REQUIRED**.
3. **CANONICAL:** Lead Lock belongs to an eligible agent’s active opportunity, lasts 48 hours, cannot be held concurrently by another eligible agent, and does not hide the property.
4. **CANONICAL:** Deal Lock starts at 7 days, may be extended to no more than 15 total days; exact eligible participant roles are **DECISION REQUIRED**.
5. **CANONICAL:** Permanent ban is a human/Super Admin decision. AI can detect, flag, recommend and escalate only.
6. **FUTURE:** Resource scope, deny-by-default RLS and server-side authorization must enforce this contract in later phases. Current client checks are not evidence of enforcement.
7. **DECISION REQUIRED:** Organization, branch, verification, subscription and privileged financial policies remain open in [NEXUS_DECISION_REGISTER_v1.0.md](./NEXUS_DECISION_REGISTER_v1.0.md).
